# Revision queue

One row per problem still in rotation. Re-solve on the `Next due` date from a blank file, 10-minute cap. Update `Clean` with a running count of consecutive clean re-solves; at 2, move the row to the Graduated section. A failed re-solve sets `Clean` to 0 and `Next due` to tomorrow.

Intervals from the original solve date: +1, +3, +7, +21.
