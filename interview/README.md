## Files

- `hard-questions.md` — the eight questions you will definitely be asked, with honest 60–90 s answers. Drafted Day 14, rehearsed Day 52.
- `behavioural/stories.md` — 8–10 STAR stories from your real work, each with a 90 s spoken version. Batch 1 Day 21, batch 2 Day 46.
- `company-research/<company>.md` — per real interview: loop format, JD mapped to your level, three exposure points.
- `salary-negotiation.md` — Day 53.
- Question banks per module live in each module's `interview-qa.md`.
